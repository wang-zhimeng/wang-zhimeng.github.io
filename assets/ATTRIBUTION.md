# Asset sources

## Johns Hopkins Blue Jay

`jhu-blue-jay.png` is the official full-color Blue Jay Body (Side View) mark, copied unchanged from the Johns Hopkins University Blue Jay Marks package.

- Announcement: https://brand.jhu.edu/blog/introducing-new-blue-jay-and-mascot-spirit-marks/
- Download: https://brand.jhu.edu/download-resources/blue-jay-marks/
- Usage guidelines: https://brand.jhu.edu/visual-identity/university-marks/

The university's trademark and usage restrictions apply. The image retains its original colors, proportions, and trademark notation. It is a separate supporting graphic, not this personal site's logo.
