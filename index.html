Reliable, decentralized control for power systems in transition.

01Research

From local device behavior to system-level guarantees.

Modern power grids are increasingly shaped by inverter-based resources. I study how device models, network conditions, and decentralized decision-making can be combined to certify stability without relying on a single centralized model.

01

Decentralized certificates

Conditions that devices or local regions can verify while supporting a global stability conclusion.

02

Network-aware analysis

Understanding how grid state and interconnection structure shape the strength of local guarantees.

03

Renewable-rich grids

Models and methods for power systems with significant inverter-based generation.
